Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

StudyNotion (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates an online learning platform that lets students enroll in courses and instructors create and publish course content. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using StudyNotion, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, password (stored hashed), and account type (student, instructor, or admin).
  • Profile information: profile picture and other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Learning activity: course enrollments, progress, ratings, and reviews.
  • Payment information: when you purchase a course, payment is processed by Razorpay. We receive payment confirmation details (such as order and payment identifiers); we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages you send through Contact Us or similar forms, and emails we send for account verification (OTP), password reset, and transactional notices.
  • Technical data: authentication tokens and basic usage data needed to operate the service (for example, data stored in your browser’s local storage while you are signed in).

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • Deliver courses, track progress, and support instructor tools
  • Process payments and send related confirmations
  • Send verification codes, password resets, and service emails
  • Respond to support or contact requests
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the platform

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us run StudyNotion, including:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers
  • Media storage (for example, Cloudinary for images and course media)
  • Payment processors (Razorpay)
  • Email delivery services used for OTPs and transactional mail

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of StudyNotion and its users.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage (such as local storage) to keep you signed in and remember session-related data. You can clear this data through your browser settings, which may sign you out of the platform.

5. Data retention

We retain account and related data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request account deletion through your account settings where available, or by contacting us.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords and authenticated API access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s privacy

StudyNotion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Update profile details from your account settings
  • Reset your password using the forgot-password flow
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us

9. Third-party links and integrations

StudyNotion may link to third-party sites or use third-party services (for example payment or media providers). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Please review those policies when you use those services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of StudyNotion after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us through the Contact Us page on StudyNotion.
